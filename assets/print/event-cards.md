# Event Cards — Cut along the lines

---

**SUPPLY DELAY** | Event
Upgrades cost +1 coin this round.
_"The shipment's late. Again."_

---

**BOOM TOWN** | Event
Every player gains 1 coin.
_"Trade is good. Everyone's eating."_

---

**STORM** | Event
Every player pays 1 coin or loses 1 Rep.
_"Batten down the hatches."_

---

**RUMOR** | Event
You lose 1 Rep -- unless someone vouches for you.
_"People are talking..."_

---

**BIG ORDER** | Event
Market prices +1 this round.
_"A caravan just arrived with deep pockets."_

---

**FESTIVAL OF PLENTY** | Event
Next 2 Festival landings give +2 Rep instead of +1.
_"The whole town is celebrating."_

---

**SWINDLE** | Event
Force one voucher redemption NOW.
_"Time to collect."_

---

**WINDFALL** | Event
You gain 3 coins.
_"Lucky day."_

**DROUGHT** | Event
No Market purchases this round.
_"Nothing on the shelves."_

**TRUST CRISIS** | Event
Players with Rep < 3 lose 1 more Rep.
_"When trust is low, it falls further."_

---

**LOST WALLET** | Event
You can't trade this turn -- unless someone lends you 1 coin.
_"Has anyone seen a small leather pouch?"_

---

**GOOD NEWS TRAVELS** | Event
If you helped someone last round, gain 2 coins now.
_"Word got around about what you did."_

**AWKWARD FAVOR** | Event

**FOUND A SHORTCUT** | Event

_"Nobody saw... right?"_

**COMMUNITY DINNER** | Event

_"Bring something to share."_

**OLD FRIEND** | Event

_"It's been too long."_

---

**BROKEN BRIDGE** | Event

_"Road's out. Might as well make camp."_

**HARVEST MOON** | Event

_"The land provides for those who need it most."_

**TALL TALE** | Event

_"Some stories are too good to be true."_

**LUCKY FIND** | Event

_"What's this?"_

**BUMPER HARVEST** | Event

**LOGGING BAN** | Event

**FEAST DAY** | Event
